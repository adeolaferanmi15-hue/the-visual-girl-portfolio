Brand identity

Your brand should be recognisable before the name is read.

A strong brand is more than a logo. Its colours, typography, imagery and visual style should work together consistently so people can recognise the brand wherever they see it.

ColourA defined palette creates recognition and keeps content visually connected.
TypographyConsistent fonts create a distinct visual voice across every touchpoint.
ImageryA clear image style gives the brand a recognisable look and feel.
ConsistencyThe goal is a brand that feels like the same brand everywhere.
